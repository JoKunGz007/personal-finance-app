# Product context

Private Ledger is a quiet, local-first financial workspace for one technically careful owner. Its single job is to turn a supported Krungthai statement into a reconciled, reviewable ledger without exposing the statement file or its password.

The product surface is an application, not a marketing dashboard. English interface copy preserves searchable Thai source text. The user should always know what remains local, which facts are immutable, where reconciliation breaks, and whether the encrypted backup is current.

Current development uses invented data only. Real statement smoke testing requires renewed explicit approval after local database, privacy, browser, and recovery acceptance passes.
