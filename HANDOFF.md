- **Direct access to `private-statements/`: granted 2026-07-25, both gates satisfied, and USED on 2026-07-26.** `scripts/mask-statement.mjs` was run against the SCB and KBANK folders, producing the 15 dumps in `masked-dumps/`, and an agent read those dumps to derive both layouts. The boundary held: no statement was opened or listed by an agent, and no value from one reached a session. The owner offered three times; the first two were declined. The grant came with a correction they accepted: they believed agent reads keep data on-device, which is false — the app's on-device guarantee (D-003) belongs to the app, not to an agent, whose reads are transmitted and retained in a session transcript. If a future session finds the owner reasoning from the older belief, correct it before acting on this authorization. The two gates are now written: **D-035** records the grant, its boundary and its cost, and `docs/FIXTURE_POLICY.md` § Masked structural dumps carries the amendment. The boundary is **invoke, don't read**: an agent may run `scripts/mask-statement.mjs` against a file in that directory and may read the dump it writes to the gitignored `masked-dumps/`; an agent may not open, list, or read the PDF itself. Once folder access exists that is discipline, not structure — say so plainly rather than implying otherwise. Statement passwords are the owner's date of birth and citizen ID — identity-grade and non-rotatable. Never accept one in chat, in a repo file, or as a CLI argument; the harness reads stdin only and needs it once per file. A dump is working material, never a fixture and never committed.
- **Reading real statements directly: granted 2026-07-28 (D-049), and narrower than it sounds.** `private-statements/` stays closed. What is open is `shared-statements/` — copies the owner produced with `scripts/repassword-pdfs.py`, carrying no password. The protection is that he first rotated his own archive off the bank password with the tool's `archive` mode, so the DOB/citizen-ID password no longer opens anything on the drive; encrypting the copies on top would have protected nothing from an agent that must be told the password to read them. He was told again that agent reads are transmitted and that a statement's *header* is the identifying part, and confirmed anyway — respect that, do not re-litigate it each session. Scope is **statement contents only**: not passwords, keys, `.env*`, backups or the citizen ID. Subagents stay closed; reads route through the parent. The rule that survives intact: reading a real value never licenses writing one into a fixture, doc, commit or screenshot.

The gap was ever only a handful of boilerplate label strings, and reading the statement would have put real transactions and counterparties into a session transcript, made "invented" unfalsifiable for every fixture written afterwards, and invited tuning the parser to one document. `docs/FIXTURE_POLICY.md` remains in force and unamended.
