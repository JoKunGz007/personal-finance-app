## What works well — keep

- All 5 typefaces and 5 colour schemes: digits always in the figures font, no contrast failures, no width change; no sideways scroll at 375px on any page.
- Focus is a visible 2.4px amber outline; tab order is logical; Escape closes notes and returns focus to the toggle.
- Money in is green and unsigned, money out red with "−", everywhere.
- `/import`'s disabled-button reason and phone stepper ("Step 1 of 5"); Shopee card action buttons are at least 47px on phone.
