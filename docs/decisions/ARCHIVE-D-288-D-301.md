## D-297 — Backup versions: a drift test holds `restore_backup`'s version list and table ladder to `lib/backup-contract.ts`; the one-record TS refactor is refused (Open item 7 #11)

- Date: 2026-10-23

- **What was found.** Backup versions and each version's tables are written by hand in two places. In TS, `lib/backup-contract.ts` holds the kind lists, the supported versions, `backupTableKindsFor` and the per-version Zod unions. In SQL, migration 055's `restore_backup` holds the version allowlist and the `v_schema>=N` kind ladder. They agree today: 49 tables, v15 sharing v14's, v16 adding seven Shopee tables. Nothing compared them directly; drift showed only through the RPC restore tests, which skip v2, v3 and v14. `tests/backup.test.ts`'s per-version list also left out v6. `private.line_inbox_items` is outside backup on purpose (migration 043's header).

It checks that the allowlist equals `SUPPORTED_BACKUP_SCHEMA_VERSIONS`, ends at `BACKUP_SCHEMA_VERSION`, and yields `backupTableKindsFor(v)` in order for every version. It needs no database. A layout it cannot parse fails loudly. v6 joins the per-version list.
- **Refused: one `BACKUP_VERSIONS` record deriving the TS unions.** It would remove about 11 hand edits per version bump, but only on the TS side. ## D-296 — A pick never starts while a correction form is open: both "Choose a statement row" buttons disable on `correcting` (Open item 7 #9)

- Date: 2026-10-23
- Status: **Built, pushed and live** (`e01ea09`). On production 2026-10-23, with one slip correction form open, all 37 "Choose a statement row" buttons were disabled; with none open, none were. The form was closed without saving. UI guard only: no route, RPC, migration or stored-value change.
- **The question was whether the Correct buttons' different disable conditions are a bug.** They are not, as the owner sees them. During a slip pick the table shows only that slip and the statement rows on offer, and during a card pick only that card and its rows (`lib/ledger-view.ts`), so cash rows never show during a pick and cards never show during a slip pick. That makes cash's missing pick guard and the card's extra slip-pick guard unreachable. The statement row's controls are hidden during a pick on purpose ("one question at a time", D-069).
- **The gap ran the other way.** "Choose a statement row" on slips and cards did not check `modes.correcting`, and `chooseRowForSlip`/`chooseRowForCard` leave it set. So a pick could start with a form open. If that form was the matched slip's own, its "Stop correcting" toggle was disabled, and saving a new amount would re-derive the rows on offer under the open chooser. If it was another record's form, that form disappeared until the pick ended. No wrong money resulted.
- **Decision:** a pick and a correction are never open at once. Both Choose buttons disable while `correcting` is set; the owner closes the form first. Clearing `correcting` on Choose was rejected: it would silently discard what the owner had typed. The per-kind Correct guards stay as they are, since the unreachable ones cost nothing and removing them would only matter if the picking view changed.
- Evidence: `tests/ledger-row-controls.test.tsx` (2; red-proved on each row separately). Traced by the investigator, then checked against `app/ledger-slip-row.tsx`, `app/ledger-card-row.tsx` and `app/transactions-view.tsx`. Not exercised in the browser. `pnpm verify` green (lint 0 errors, tsc, docs, build); Vitest 105 files (1,986 passed / 19 skipped).

The 500s are still undiagnosed; a retry can hide one, and the log line is the trace.

Deposits never enter, so a refund does not reduce a category. Excluded rows (transfers, duplicates) drop out through `include_in_reporting`; no category means one "Uncategorised" bucket; machine ("auto") categories count like owner ones. `GET /api/v1/statistics/categories` shares `statisticsWindowSchema` with `/api/v1/statistics`. `groupSpendingByParent` rolls children under their parent in BigInt: a group's share is of all spending, a child's of its group.
